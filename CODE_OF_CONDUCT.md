# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and productive for everyone.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and give clear, constructive feedback.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Yuvraj Singh Chauhan, through the contact details on their GitHub profile (https://github.com/ysinghc).
